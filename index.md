## Welcome

I am **Junteng Liu** (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at **HKUST NLP Group** under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a Bachelor of Engineering degree.

### Research Interests

My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with specific interests in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

### Current Position

I am currently a **Research Intern at MINIMAX** (February 2025 - Present), where I am conducting research in natural language processing and machine learning.

### Previous Experience

Previously, I have worked as a Research Intern at:
- **Tencent WXG** (June 2024 - September 2024) - Advisor: Zifei Shan
- **Shanghai AI Lab** (June 2023 - December 2023) - Advisor: Professor Yu Cheng
