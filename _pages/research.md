## Research Interests

My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with the following specific interests:

### LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning

Investigating how large language models can be improved through reasoning and reinforcement learning techniques. This includes:
- Logical reasoning capabilities
- Step-by-step problem solving
- Reward modeling and optimization

### Hallucination in Vision-Language Models

Studying the phenomenon of hallucinations in vision-language models, particularly in scenarios such as:
- Chart understanding and interpretation
- Visual question answering
- Multimodal reasoning tasks

### LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

Exploring methods to improve the truthfulness and interpretability of large language models:
- Identifying and mitigating false information
- Understanding internal representations
- Developing interpretability tools
